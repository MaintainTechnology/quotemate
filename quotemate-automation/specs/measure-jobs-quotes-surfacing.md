# Measure-tool jobs on the Quotes surfaces + Tradie measurement view polish

## Title
Surface measurement results (measure-tool jobs) on the Quotes tab and every trade hub's quotes sub-section, with CTAs to the measurement results page, the tradie view, and the customer view — and polish the `/m/[token]` tradie measurement view and the roofing full-quote tradie surface to the Command Centre design system.

## Goal
A tradie who saves a job from ANY measure tool (roofing, solar, painting, commercial-paint, aircon, signage where applicable) finds that job on the Quotes tab AND on that trade's hub quotes section within one click of the dashboard, with working "Measurement results / Tradie view" and "Customer page" CTAs — and the `/m/[token]` page reads as the same product as the customer quote page (Command Centre tokens, sheet layout, zero off-system styling).

## Role
Principal product designer + design engineer. Act autonomously on reversible edits; follow the repo's pure-helper + colocated-vitest idiom; minimal diffs, root-cause fixes; never break the customer-facing surfaces.
