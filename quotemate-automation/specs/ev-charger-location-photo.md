# EV charger location photo — Spec

> Companions: `specs/ev-charger-job-quoter.md` (the dashboard form),
> `specs/ev-charger-sms-auto-quote.md` (the SMS auto-quote fix),
> `specs/ev-charger-estimate-template.md` (the estimate document, shipped).
> Fleet targets: `qm-electrical-receptionist` (generated by
> `scripts/export-receptionist.mjs`) and `qm-front-desk` (hand-maintained).

## Objective

Let an EV charger customer send a photo of where the charger is going, and put
that photo — plus an AI render of the charger installed in that spot — into the
quote they receive. The photo is **optional** on the tradie dashboard form and
**required** over SMS, where the receptionist must also ask every question the
form asks. For electrical tradies quoting EV charger jobs, and their customers.

## Context — most of this already exists

Verified in code and against production on 2026-09-03. The feature is much
smaller than it appears; four of the five pieces are already built and simply
never reached by an EV job.

**The AI render is built.** `generatePreviewImage(quoteId)`
([lib/ig-engine/generate.ts:79](lib/ig-engine/generate.ts:79)) already takes the
customer's photos from `intakes.photo_paths`, renders an "after" image through
**Gemini Nano Banana** — `GEMINI_IMAGE_MODEL ?? 'gemini-3-pro-image'`
([lib/ig-engine/providers/gemini.ts:32](lib/ig-engine/providers/gemini.ts:32)) —
and stores the results on `quotes.preview_image_paths` behind an atomic
claim and a status machine (`idle | no_photos | generating | ready | failed |
partial`). It is job-type generic, already fires from three triggers
(`/api/upload/[token]` :264, `/api/estimate/draft` :778, `/q/[token]` :649), and
`lib/ig-engine/prompts.ts` already carries EV vocabulary (count 1, noun
"EV charger", default room "garage or driveway" — :126, :139, :173).

**The document already renders images.** `resolveEvImages`
([lib/quote/pdf.ts:806](lib/quote/pdf.ts:806)) reads `intakes.photo_paths` and
prints up to `EV_MAX_IMAGES = 3` through `imagesSection`
([lib/quote/report-html-ev-charger.ts:523](lib/quote/report-html-ev-charger.ts:523)).

**MMS photos are already captured.** `extractAndStoreMmsPhotos`
([lib/sms/mms.ts:39](lib/sms/mms.ts:39)) stores any inbound picture for any job
type, and `structureIntake` consumes them as vision input.

**So the only real gap is that an EV job never gets a photo in the first place.**
Production confirms it: **5 EV intakes exist, 0 carry a photo.** The dashboard
form has no file input anywhere (`grep type="file"` over `app/dashboard/job/**`
returns nothing) and the SMS photo-link is hard-blocked for EV —
`shouldSendPhotoRequest` refuses with `job_type_not_easy5`
([lib/sms/photo-request-trigger.ts:79](lib/sms/photo-request-trigger.ts:79))
because `EASY_5_JOB_TYPES` excludes `ev_charger`, and
[lib/sms/dialog.ts:1645](lib/sms/dialog.ts:1645) emits
`PHOTO LINK STATE: not_applicable … Do not mention photos.`

**Question parity is 5 versus 3.** The dashboard asks five things
([lib/quote/job-fields.ts:189-231](lib/quote/job-fields.ts:189)): vehicle,
charger_supply, room, switchboard_distance, phase. SMS asks three, and they live
in the **database**, not code — `shared_assemblies.clarifying_questions` on the
`Install EV charger` row (id `52f354d2…`, verified in prod: `category='ev_charger'`,
`always_inspection=false`, `default_enabled=false`, 3 questions):

1. "Is the charger on-site, and which model is it?"
2. "Roughly how far is the parking spot from the switchboard?"
3. "Single or three-phase supply, and any idea of spare switchboard capacity?"

**Two live defects make "required" dangerous today.** Both must be fixed before
adding questions, or this feature makes EV conversion worse:

- **The cap fires on cooperative customers.** Every EV question returns the same
  fact code `'service_question'`
  ([lib/sms/quote-readiness.ts:408](lib/sms/quote-readiness.ts:408)), and the
  route's progress check compares *array lengths* of missing codes
  ([app/api/sms/inbound/route.ts:3665](app/api/sms/inbound/route.ts:3665)). On a
  pure EV flow the missing set is `['service_question']` on every blocked turn,
  the length never shrinks, `madeProgress` is always false, and
  `clarify_gate_count` increments even while the customer answers each question
  in order. At the cap (default 6) the turn is rewritten to
  `escalate_inspection` and the job becomes a $99 site visit.
- **7 of 8 tenants gate on nothing.** The row is `default_enabled=false` and
  production shows **exactly 1 tenant** has enabled it. For the other seven,
  `findMatchedService` returns null and an EV finish sails through with zero
  job-specific facts. "Required" that only applies to one tenant is not required.

**What the customer supplies changes what may be said.** Production has **zero**
`tenant_material_catalogue` rows in category `ev_charger` — no tenant stocks a
charger. So the WP9 product offer always returns null, there is never a product
image, and the customer's own photo is the only image an EV quote can carry.
Naming a charger model is safe **only in prose**: a priced line naming it hits
`looksLikeEvChargerUnit` ([lib/estimate/ev-charger-supply.ts:120](lib/estimate/ev-charger-supply.ts:120))
with no catalogue anchor and fails the **whole quote** closed to $99 inspection,
and `docs/strategy.md` v22 forbids inferring a charger price outright.

## Decisions taken (the request had three ambiguities)

The request said "a photo of the **location**" twice and "the uploaded photo of
the **EV charger**" once. These are resolved as follows; each is an open question
if the reading is wrong.

- **D1 — One upload, purpose "location".** The customer photographs the spot
  where the charger goes. If their own charger unit is visible, that is a bonus,
  not a second required upload. The control and the SMS copy both say "the spot
  where the charger will go".
- **D2 — The AI image renders the charger INTO the location photo.** The
  customer's photo is the source image; the render shows an EV charger mounted
  in that spot. This is exactly what `generatePreviewImage` already does for
  other job types.
- **D3 — "The type of EV charger depicted" is PROSE ONLY.** Where a model is
  identifiable it may appear in `scope_of_works`, Description of Works or an
  assumption. It must **never** become a line item, carry a price, or influence
  pricing. This is forced by the supply fence, the grounding validator's
  `ev_charger` category tag ([lib/estimate/validate.ts:175](lib/estimate/validate.ts:175))
  and strategy v22.

## Requirements

### A — The dashboard form

**R1 — An optional photo control on the EV charger form.** Add a file input to
`app/dashboard/job/_components/JobQuoteForm.tsx`, rendered **only** when
`jobType === 'ev_charger'`, between the address block and the Notes textarea
(:518). Label: "Photo of the spot (optional)". Accept `image/jpeg`, `image/png`,
`image/webp`; at most **3** files; at most **8 MB** each. Copy the existing
control and uploader from
[app/quote-request/[token]/QuoteRequestForm.tsx:120-141, :346-355](app/quote-request/[token]/QuoteRequestForm.tsx:120)
— including its rule that **an upload failure never blocks submit**.

**R2 — A tenant-authed upload route.** Add `POST /api/tenant/job-quote/photos`
accepting `multipart/form-data`. Gate it with the same bearer path the job-quote
route uses (`requireFeature` / `resolveTenantRequest`,
[lib/features/guard.ts:31](lib/features/guard.ts:31)) — this is a dashboard
surface, so it must NOT be token-only like the public form's route. Validate MIME
and size server-side (never trust the client), store through
`uploadIntakePhoto` ([lib/storage/upload.ts:22](lib/storage/upload.ts:22)) into
the private `intake-photos` bucket, and return `{ paths: string[], urls: string[] }`.
`runtime = 'nodejs'`, `dynamic = 'force-dynamic'`, mirroring
[app/api/tenant/catalogue/upload/route.ts](app/api/tenant/catalogue/upload/route.ts).

**R3 — Carry the photos onto the intake.** The form uploads first, then submits
the returned paths. Extend the job-quote `BodySchema`
([app/api/tenant/job-quote/route.ts:47](app/api/tenant/job-quote/route.ts:47))
with `photo_paths?: string[]` and `photo_urls?: string[]` (max 3, validated), and
write both onto the `intakes` insert (:260-283, which writes neither today).
Also pass the signed URLs as the **second argument** to `structureIntake`
(:144) — that parameter is `photoUrls: string[]` and is currently always `[]`,
so the structurer's vision already accepts them with no signature change.

### B — The SMS receptionist

**R4 — Fix the progress bug before adding any question.** `missingServiceQuestion`
must return a fact code that identifies **which** question is outstanding (e.g.
`service_question:<index>` or a stable slug), and the route's `madeProgress`
comparison must treat a *changed* missing set as progress, not only a shorter
one. Without this, R5 makes EV conversion strictly worse. A test must prove that
a customer answering five questions in order never increments
`clarify_gate_count`.

**R5 — SMS asks everything the form asks.** Update
`shared_assemblies.clarifying_questions` for `name = 'Install EV charger'` to
five questions matching the form's five fields, in the form's order, via a new
numbered migration (with `_down.sql` and a runner, following the idempotent,
name-keyed, emptiness-guarded pattern of migration 121). Suggested text, each
carrying a ≥4-character topic noun so `serviceKeywords`
([lib/sms/quote-readiness.ts:440](lib/sms/quote-readiness.ts:440)) can match it:

1. "Which electric vehicle is the charger for — Tesla, BYD, or another make?"
2. "Do you already have the charger unit, or should we supply it?"
3. "Whereabouts is the charger going — garage, carport, or an external wall?"
4. "Roughly how far is the charger spot from the switchboard?"
5. "Is the property single phase or three phase, and any idea of spare switchboard capacity?"

Five stays under `MAX_MUSTASK_PER_SERVICE = 6`
([lib/sms/dialog.ts:1478](lib/sms/dialog.ts:1478)); a sixth text question would
exceed the prompt render while the gate still enforced it, so **do not add one**.

**R6 — The photo is a required sixth step, gated separately.** `quote-readiness.ts`
has no photo, image or MMS awareness, so this cannot ride on
`clarifying_questions`. Add a distinct readiness fact (code `ev_photo`) that is
satisfied when the conversation has at least one stored photo — either an inbound
MMS (`sms_messages.photo_paths`) or an upload through the link
(`sms_conversations.photo_paths`). It is evaluated **only** for
`job_type = 'ev_charger'`, and **only after** all five text questions are
answered, so the customer is never asked for a photo before the job is understood.

**R7 — Let the EV photo link actually send.** Add `ev_charger` to the photo-link
eligibility path so `shouldSendPhotoRequest`
([lib/sms/photo-request-trigger.ts:79](lib/sms/photo-request-trigger.ts:79)) stops
returning `job_type_not_easy5`, and to the Rule 10 eligibility list
([lib/sms/dialog.ts:1641](lib/sms/dialog.ts:1641)) so the model is told the link
is available instead of `not_applicable`. The existing one-shot guard
(`photo_request_sent_at`) is unchanged. EV must **not** be added to
`EASY_5_JOB_TYPES` itself — that set drives assumption rules and pricing
behaviour, not just photos.

**R8 — Photo request copy for EV says required, not optional.** The existing
photo-request text offers the photo as optional. For `ev_charger` it must read as
required and say why, e.g. "Last thing — send a photo of the spot where the
charger will go and I'll get your quote across. Tap here: <link>". No emoji, no
em-dash, Australian English.

**R9 — An explicit escape when the customer cannot send a photo.** If the
customer declines or says they cannot (matching the existing `DECLINE_RE`
vocabulary plus "can't take", "no camera", "not there", "not at the property"),
the `ev_photo` fact is satisfied-by-decline exactly once, the conversation
proceeds to a quote, and the intake records `photo_declined = true` in
`scope.specs`. Without this, a customer who is not at the property is looped to
the cap and converted to a $99 inspection — the opposite of the intent.

**R10 — Close the default_enabled hole.** An EV finish must be gated on all 8
tenants, not the 1 that has enabled the catalogue row. When
`findMatchedService` finds no enabled EV service but `job_type === 'ev_charger'`,
fall back to the same five questions from a code-side constant so the MUST-ASK
set still applies. The DB row stays the source of truth when it IS enabled.

**R11 — Do not let a rephrased question be discarded by grounding.**
`assertGroundedReply` rejects any figure ≥ 10 not present in the corpus
([lib/sms/llm-receptionist.ts:233](lib/sms/llm-receptionist.ts:233)), so a model
rephrasing question 4 with "5-10 m" or "over 10 m" can be thrown away and
replaced by the snag fallback. Either add the question text to the grounding
corpus ([app/api/sms/inbound/route.ts:3524](app/api/sms/inbound/route.ts:3524)) or
keep the distance question free of numerals. The deterministic gate's own reply
is already safe (assigned after grounding runs).

### C — The quote document and the AI render

**R12 — Render the AI image in the EV estimate.** `resolveEvImages`
([lib/quote/pdf.ts:806](lib/quote/pdf.ts:806)) must also read
`quotes.preview_image_paths` and include the render, captioned "Indicative — how
the charger could look in this spot". Order: the AI render first, then the
customer's own photo(s), then the tenant product shot if one exists. The
`EV_MAX_IMAGES = 3` cap, the 640px `prepareImage` limit and the
`embeddedImageMissing` retry are unchanged.

**R13 — The document must never trigger a render.** Reading only. Per
`specs/ev-charger-estimate-template.md` R14, no billable AI call may originate
from a PDF render or a document request. The render is produced by the existing
draft-time and upload-time triggers. If `preview_status` is not `ready` or
`partial`, the document simply prints what it has.

**R14 — Bump the template cache key.** The document's output changes, so
`EV_ESTIMATE_TEMPLATE_KEY` must go `'ev1'` → `'ev2'`
([lib/quote/report-html-ev-charger.ts:44](lib/quote/report-html-ev-charger.ts:44)),
and the image set must be folded into `quotePdfSignature` so a newly-arrived
render invalidates a cached PDF rather than serving one without it.

**R15 — EV-specific render prompt.** The image prompt must instruct a wall-mounted
EV charger installed in the photographed location, keeping the customer's
background intact. It must not depict a brand, a price, a person, or a vehicle
charging unless one is already in the photo. Where the charger model is known
from the answers it may be described generically ("a compact wall-mounted
charger"), never as a named branded product — an AI-drawn "Tesla Wall Connector"
is a representation the tradie may not be able to honour.

**R16 — Prose identification only (D3).** If vision identifies the customer's
existing charger, that may appear in `scope_of_works`, Description of Works or an
assumption. It must never appear as a line item, carry a price, or reach
`lookup_material`.

> **Amended 2026-09-04 (build review).** This first demanded a test asserting
> that *no priced line matches `looksLikeEvChargerUnit`*. That invariant is
> false, and asserting it would fail against a legitimate draft:
> `enforceEvChargerCustomerSupplyFence` skips protected lines
> (`isProtectedInstallationLine` — labour, callout, sundry, `assembly:*`,
> `tradie_manual`) **before** the detector runs, so a priced assembly line
> reading "Install Tesla Wall Connector (customer supplied)" legitimately
> survives at full price and matches the detector. The detector is an
> ambiguity tripwire for *unanchored material* lines, not a naming ban.
>
> The testable invariant is the one that actually protects the money: **no
> photo-derived text may reach a priced MATERIAL line.** Assert instead that a
> customer-supplied EV draft carrying a charger name only in `scope_of_works` /
> assumptions produces no `material:`-sourced line matching
> `looksLikeEvChargerUnit`, and that an unanchored one still fails the whole
> quote closed to inspection (the existing fence behaviour).

## Non-goals

- Adding `ev_charger` to `EASY_5_JOB_TYPES`, or changing assumption rules,
  pricing behaviour or the $99 model for any trade.
- Seeding charger-unit prices or catalogue rows. Production has zero and that
  stays Jon's data (strategy v22).
- Pricing anything from a photo, or letting vision influence a figure.
- A photo requirement for any trade or job type other than EV charger.
- Changing the roofing, painting or solar preview pipelines, or
  `painting_measurements.preview_*`.
- A new image provider, model or `ig-engine` rewrite — Nano Banana
  (`gemini-3-pro-image`) is already the default.
- Any change to `qm-front-desk`.
- Backfilling previews for the 5 existing EV intakes.

## Constraints

- **Money stays deterministic.** No figure may derive from a photo or a render.
  The supply fence, the grounding validator and strategy v22 all bind.
- **The render is billable.** It runs at draft/upload time behind the existing
  atomic claim, never on a PDF or document request, and never more than once per
  quote unless the photo set changes.
- **`supabase-js` resolves `{data, error}`** — every new write checks `error`.
  A photo failure must never fail a quote or an SMS send.
- **The SMS turn budget is real.** The reply cap is 320 characters
  ([lib/sms/dialog.ts:48](lib/sms/dialog.ts:48)) and the clarify cap defaults to
  6 turns. Five questions plus a photo step is six blocking turns — R4 is what
  makes that survivable.
- New `lib/` modules must be reachable from the three exported routes or the
  carve-out silently omits them.
- Australian English, no emoji, no em-dashes in customer-facing copy.
- Existing suites stay green: `pnpm test`, `pnpm typecheck`,
  `node --import tsx scripts/test-sms-parity.mjs`.

## Edge cases to handle

- Customer sends a photo of something irrelevant → still stored and rendered; no
  vision gate rejects it. The tradie sees it on review.
- Customer sends 6 photos by MMS → the first 3 reach the document
  (`EV_MAX_IMAGES`); all are stored.
- Photo arrives *before* all five questions are answered → accepted and counted;
  the `ev_photo` fact is already satisfied when its turn comes.
- Customer declines the photo (R9) → quote proceeds, `photo_declined` recorded,
  never asked again in that conversation.
- `GEMINI_API_KEY` missing or the render fails → `preview_status` becomes
  `failed`, the document prints the customer's raw photo only, the quote and SMS
  are unaffected.
- Render still `generating` when the PDF is built → document omits it; the
  signature change (R14) means the next download picks it up.
- Three-phase answer → `inspection_required = true` and terminal
  ([lib/estimate/run.ts:185](lib/estimate/run.ts:185)); no PDF exists, so no
  images and no render. Unchanged.
- Tenant has not enabled the EV service → R10 fallback still asks all five.
- Dashboard upload succeeds but the form submit fails → orphan objects in the
  bucket. Accepted; they are unreferenced and cheap.
- Dashboard upload fails → submit proceeds without photos (R1), no error blocks
  the tradie.
- The same quote is opened twice while generating → the existing atomic claim
  makes the second call a no-op.
- Customer supplies the charger and it is visible in the photo → prose may name
  it (R16); Exclusions still carry "Supply of the EV charger unit itself."
