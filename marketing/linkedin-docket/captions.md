# QuoteMax LinkedIn, docket set

15 standalone posts, 1080x1350. Graphics in `out/post-01.png` to `out/post-15.png`.

Each graphic carries its own reference code in the header rail (`QM-2607-NN`) and its
brief on the right (`AD`, `PAIN`, `EDU`, `LEAD`), so a post can be matched back to this
file at a glance.

## House rules for anything added

- Australian English. Colour, organise, licence (noun), tradie, sparky, on the tools.
- No exclamation marks. No em dashes. No emoji. Middots and arrows only.
- No fabricated stats and no invented testimonials. Every number here is a real product
  fact: under a minute, 24/7, $0 commission, $99 site visit credited back, from $49/mo,
  14-day trial on Starter Monthly, about 3 minutes to set up, 8 live trades.
- Banned words: leverage, synergy, unlock, seamless, revolutionary, supercharge,
  game-changing.
- Text on a yellow fill is `#1C1812`. Never white.

Three a week for five weeks. The numbering is already interleaved so two posts with the
same job never land back to back: AD, PAIN, PAIN, PAIN, PAIN, EDU, EDU, EDU, EDU, AD,
LEAD, LEAD, LEAD, LEAD, AD.

Posts 03 and 04 are the two to boost. The yellow before-and-after panel is the clearest
