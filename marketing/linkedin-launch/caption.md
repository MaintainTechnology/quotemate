# Launch post copy

The deck is one **document post**, so it takes one caption, not twenty. Upload
`out/quotemax-launch-carousel.pdf` and use the copy below.

## Document title

Set this in LinkedIn's document upload field. It shows above the first slide.

```
QuoteMax is live: AI quoting for Australian tradies
```

## Caption

```
QuoteMax is live in Australia.

It is a quoting line for tradies. Your customer texts your number, it asks the
questions you would ask, prices the job against your own rate book, and drafts the
quote. You review it and send it.

Under a minute, start to finish. Answered around the clock. No commission on your work.

Eight trades are live today: electrical, plumbing, roofing, solar, painting,
commercial painting, aircon and signage. Each one prices against its own book,
because a roofer and a sparky do not quote the same way.

The part worth saying plainly: the model runs the conversation, it never invents a
price. If a figure is not in your book it does not go out, and anything too complex
to price safely books a $99 site visit instead of a guess. That $99 comes back off
the job.

Plans start at $49 a month. Starter Monthly includes a 14-day free trial. About three
minutes to set up.

Twenty slides on why we built it and how it works. Swipe through, and tell me if your
trade should be next.

quotemax.com.au

#Tradies #AIQuoting #AustralianBusiness #SmallBusinessAU #Electrical #Plumbing
#Roofing #Solar #Painting #ProductLaunch
```

## First comment

LinkedIn suppresses posts with outbound links in the body less than posts without, so
if you strip the URL above, put it here instead.

```
Trades live today and where to start: quotemax.com.au
Not on the list? Comment your trade and I will line you up for the next intake.
```

## House rules

Australian English. No exclamation marks, no em dashes, no emoji. Every figure is a
real product fact. No testimonials, because there are no consented ones to quote yet.
