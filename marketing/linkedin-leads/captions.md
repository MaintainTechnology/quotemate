# Lead post captions

Six captions. One for the referral docket you sent, five for the new posts in
this folder. All single-image posts, not documents.

Every one opens with the observation, not the product. The first two lines are
what sits above "see more" on mobile, so they carry the hook alone. The product
is allowed in around line four, never before.

House rules: Australian English, no exclamation marks, no em dashes, no emoji.
Every figure is real.

## How to run them

Post one a week, in this order: **62, 61, 65, 64, 63**. That leads with the
reframe, which is the most shareable and the least like an advert, and holds the
two with the softest product mention until people already recognise the name.

Three of the five end with a question. That is deliberate and it is the main
lever you have on reach: replies from the poster in the first hour matter more
than anything in the caption itself, so leave yourself something to reply to.

Post from a personal profile and let the company page reshare. Individuals get
materially more organic reach than pages on LinkedIn.

Keep `quotemax.com.au` out of the body on the four that do not already carry it.
Put it in your own first comment instead.

One caveat: the ordering and the link advice are general LinkedIn practice, not
anything measured on your account. If you already know what your audience
responds to, trust that over this.
