The brand's closing punctuation: a yellow ticker bar with dark-ink text that loops seamlessly.

```jsx
<Marquee items={["QuoteMax", "Built in Australia", "For tradies, by tradies", "Quote drafted in under a minute"]} />
```

- Place at the foot of a marketing page or quote page. Text on yellow is always dark ink. Hover pauses; reduced-motion shows it static.
- Keep items short and uppercase; the component adds the middot separators.
