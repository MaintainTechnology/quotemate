The signature step card — a large accent mono number beside an all-caps title and body. Built on `Card`.

```jsx
<NumberedCard num="01" title="Customer texts your number"
  body="Each tradie gets a dedicated AU number. Voice or SMS, both feed QuoteMax." />
```

- Use for ordered sequences (how-it-works, onboarding). Numbers are mono, accent, and large; stack cards with a hairline timeline spine behind them for the marketing pattern.
- Pass `children` for custom body content instead of `body`.
