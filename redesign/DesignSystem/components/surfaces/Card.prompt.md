The structural surface: a palette-charcoal panel held by a warm hairline and lifted by the inner lit edge — never a drop shadow. Square corners.

```jsx
<Card>Plain panel</Card>
<Card interactive sweep>Hover warms the border + draws the accent sweep</Card>
<Card accentTop>Featured — persistent 2px accent top rule</Card>
```

- `lit` (default on) adds the inner top highlight. `interactive` + `sweep` are the marketing/dashboard hover treatment. `accentTop` marks a featured panel.
- Compose other content inside; don't add drop shadows. Use `href` to make the whole card a link.
